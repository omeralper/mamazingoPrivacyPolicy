# Mamazingo Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: June 26, 2026**

Mamazingo is an AI-powered storytelling app developed by Omer Ozkan, based in Turkey. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how optional photo uploads are handled, and what choices you have.

## Information We Collect

Mamazingo is designed to provide a creative storytelling experience for families and users of different ages. We do not require users to create an account, and we do not intentionally collect personal information such as full names, email addresses, phone numbers, or precise location data.

We may collect or process the following information:

### App Usage Data

We may collect anonymous or non-identifying usage data, such as the number of stories created, chapters read, features used, app version, device type, operating system, and technical performance information. This helps us improve the app and fix bugs.

### In-App Purchase Data

In-app purchases are handled securely by Apple App Store and RevenueCat. We do not collect or store credit card numbers or payment credentials. We may receive purchase-related information such as product ID, purchase status, entitlement status, transaction identifiers, and subscription or credit balance information in order to unlock paid features.

### Story Content

Users may provide story-related inputs such as character traits, age range, appearance preferences, interests, or choices made during the story. This information is used to generate personalized stories, voices, and illustrations. We do not use this information to identify the user.

### Optional Photo Uploads

Mamazingo may allow users to optionally upload a photo to create a more personalized story character or illustration. Uploading a photo is not required to use the app.

Uploaded photos may contain personal information, such as a person’s face, appearance, or other visual details. Photos are used only to generate or customize AI-created story images and related app content.

We do not use uploaded photos to identify users, build a biometric profile, or verify identity. We do not sell uploaded photos.

Uploaded photos may be temporarily stored on our servers or cloud storage for processing and delivery. We aim to delete uploaded photos automatically after a short period, usually within 1 hour. Generated images or story outputs may be stored in the app or backend so the user can continue their story experience.

## How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

* Provide and personalize AI-generated stories.
* Generate optional AI voices, images, and illustrations.
* Enable and verify in-app purchases.
* Maintain story progress and app functionality.
* Improve performance, reliability, and user experience.
* Detect abuse, prevent misuse, and protect the safety of the service.
* Respond to support, privacy, or deletion requests.

## Third-Party Services

Mamazingo uses trusted third-party services to operate the app:

* **Apple App Store** for in-app purchases and payment processing.
* **RevenueCat** for purchase validation, credits, and subscription or entitlement management.
* **OpenAI** for AI-generated text, image, and voice features.
* **Leonardo AI** for AI-generated image features.
* **Cloud hosting and storage providers** for temporary file storage, backend processing, and app delivery.
* **Analytics or crash reporting tools**, if enabled, to understand app performance and improve stability.

These services may process story inputs, uploaded photos, generated content, purchase data, or technical information only as needed to provide the app’s features. Each third-party service has its own privacy policy and data processing practices.

We do not knowingly send full names, email addresses, phone numbers, or precise location data to AI providers unless a user voluntarily includes such information inside a story prompt or uploaded content.

## Children’s Privacy

Mamazingo is intended to be used with appropriate parental or guardian oversight when used by children. We do not knowingly collect personal contact information from children, such as email addresses, phone numbers, or precise location data.

If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal information through the app, they may contact us and request review or deletion of the relevant information.

Parents and guardians should supervise photo uploads and avoid uploading images that contain sensitive, private, or unnecessary personal information.

## Photo and Content Safety

We may use automated moderation or safety checks to help prevent misuse of photo uploads, story prompts, and generated content. Content that violates our safety rules may be rejected or blocked.

Users should not upload photos or prompts involving nudity, sexual content, violence, self-harm, illegal activity, or content that violates the rights or privacy of others.

## Data Retention

We keep data only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the app, maintain story progress, support purchases, improve performance, comply with legal obligations, and protect the service from misuse.

Uploaded photos are intended to be temporary and are usually deleted automatically within 1 hour after processing.

Generated stories, generated images, technical logs, and purchase-related records may be retained for longer where needed to provide app functionality, restore purchases, resolve support issues, or comply with legal requirements.

## Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including secure transmission and limited access to backend systems. However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your use of Mamazingo.

Parents or guardians may contact us to request deletion of information related to their child.

Because Mamazingo does not require user accounts, we may need limited information from you to locate the relevant data, such as a device identifier, purchase identifier, story identifier, or other app-related reference.

## International Data Transfers

Mamazingo and its third-party service providers may process data in countries other than your country of residence. Where applicable, we rely on appropriate safeguards required by privacy laws.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted with a new “Effective Date.” Continued use of the app after an update means the updated policy applies.
